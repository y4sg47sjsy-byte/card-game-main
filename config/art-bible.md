# WANIN IP Art Bible

Status: `smoke_test`

## Smoke-test scope

- The production IP is WANIN.
- This direction applies only to the `gummy-king-001`, `gummy-queen-001`, and `gummy-dragon-001` pipeline validation and is not a general WANIN canon definition.
- Each card's first listed reference is its primary identity reference.
- All other listed references contribute series-level material, shape, highlight, lighting, detail-density, and composition language only. They must not contribute another character's identity features.

## Minimum visual direction

- High-saturation, smooth, rounded 3D candy/gummy rendering.
- Bright candy colors, strong glossy highlights, and soft polished mobile-game promotional lighting.
- Cute, joyful, exaggerated, all-ages presentation with a clear jackpot celebration.
- Keep a polished mobile-game collectible-card finish: one dominant character, clean silhouette, moderate controlled detail density, and a legible foreground/midground/background hierarchy.
- The card's primary character remains the dominant subject at roughly 60–65% of the template's 654×560 (about 7:6) illustration window.
- Use a slightly low heroic camera angle while keeping every identity-critical head feature and the major body silhouette visible.
- Keep identity-critical features inside the center-crop-safe region; leave at least 12–15% horizontal safety margin when generation uses a wider source canvas.
- Use soft warm key light, cool or candy-colored edge light, strong rounded glossy highlights, and luminous but never dark backgrounds consistently across the series.
- The candy palace, treasure, coins, candy, and gem-like gummies support the scene without competing with the character.

## Prohibited drift

- No card frame, text, logo, watermark, or UI.
- No realistic fur, scales, realistic animal anatomy, ordinary toy treatment, or human proportions.
- Do not change the primary character's reference-defined identity colors, anatomy, costume, or accessories.
- Do not mix identity features between characters.
- Do not make the background dark.
