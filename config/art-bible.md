# X-STARS Production Art Bible

Status: `production`

## Project intent

- X-STARS is a collectible-card project about assembling a five-member virtual idol group, gathering stage Symbols, and completing Stage Combos.
- The five members are fixed identities supplied by company references. `XS_MEMBER_01` through `XS_MEMBER_05` are production placeholders, not names or canon.
- A Character Card preserves one reference-defined member identity and changes only Costume, pose, stage treatment, lighting, and rarity presentation.
- A Symbol Card is an original non-character asset and does not depend on company character references.
- Do not invent biography, personality, relationships, official colors, lore, or other WANIN canon beyond the approved card definition and visible references.

## Shared collectible-card finish

- Premium, polished virtual-idol promotional art with a bright, aspirational, all-ages tone.
- Use saturated color, controlled contrast, clean silhouettes, refined materials, and clear foreground/midground/background separation.
- Favor stage lighting, LED structures, concert haze, prismatic reflections, tasteful sparkle, and celebratory energy.
- Keep the main subject readable at thumbnail size. Effects support the subject and never cover the face or destroy the silhouette.
- Illustration contains no card frame, title, rules text, number, logo, watermark, or UI; deterministic composition adds card information later.
- Compose every illustration for the template's 654×560 (about 7:6) final art window and fixed center crop.
- Keep identity-critical features and primary Symbol silhouettes inside the center-crop-safe region, with at least 12–15% horizontal safety margin when using a wider source canvas.

## Character Card direction

- The card's listed character reference is the primary identity authority for that member.
- Preserve the reference-defined face, facial proportions, eyes, hairstyle, hair color, skin tone, body proportions, and persistent identifying features.
- Costume transformation may change clothing, fashion accessories, performance props, and stage styling only. It must not redesign anatomy or identity.
- Use one dominant member per illustration. Do not add another member, a duplicate, a reflected duplicate face, or a clearly rendered crowd character.
- Frame the member at roughly 60–65% of the art window, normally in a medium-to-near-full-body performance pose with face and hands legible.
- Costume, pose, stage/background, lighting, and rarity feeling follow the card's `art_brief`; card-specific constraints override shared preferences.
- SR means a clearly themed costume, coordinated stage, controlled light layers, refined accessories, and premium finish without SSR-scale spectacle.
- Numeric `cost` currently displays Power / Star level only. It is not Mana, payment, combat strength, ATK, HP, or DEF.

## Symbol Card direction

- MIC, NOTE, GEM, STAR, and WILD are original stage-resource icons designed as a coherent family without character references.
- Target high-quality casual slot/casino-symbol satisfaction: bold volume, glossy premium materials, strong highlights, saturated color, immediate recognition, and reward energy, translated into an idol concert world rather than a realistic gambling venue.
- Use one large centered object or emblem at roughly 55–70% of the art window with a clean silhouette and strong value contrast.
- Share a family construction language: premium 3D object, round stage-light frame, controlled sparkle, smooth bevels, and luminous concert backdrop.
- Give each basic Symbol a distinct palette and silhouette. WILD uses the full shared palette and appears more special while remaining easy to parse.
- Do not add characters, hands, branded products, readable labels, casino tables, playing cards, chips, roulette wheels, or realistic gambling interiors.

## Rarity language

- R: clean base presentation, one clear material family, restrained stage effects.
- SR: themed styling, richer material contrast, coordinated colored lighting, and refined secondary details.
- SSR: elaborate limited costume, luxurious stage architecture, layered premium effects, and stronger spectacle while preserving identity readability.
- SP: special illustration or commemorative presentation led by collection value; it need not appear stronger than SSR.

## Prohibited drift

- No combat imagery, weapons, attacks, damage, armor-stat language, ATK, HP, DEF, or Mana motifs.
- No invented X-STARS names, lore, official traits, member relationships, or identity features.
- No identity mixing and no replacement of a reference-defined face or hairstyle with a generic idol design.
- No sexualized framing, unsafe age ambiguity, horror, injury, oppressive darkness, or photorealistic gambling environments.
- No generated card layout elements. Illustration generation and deterministic card composition remain separate stages.
